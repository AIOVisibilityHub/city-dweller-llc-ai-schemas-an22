Wood Siding Replacement | City Dweller, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-05

Type: CollectionPage

City Dweller, LLC offers expert wood siding replacement services to revitalize your home's exterior and provide long-term protection. When wood siding has reached the end of its lifespan or suffered extensive damage, a full or partial replacement is necessary to keep your home safe. City Dweller, LLC provides professional wood siding replacement throughout the San Francisco Bay Area. We help you choose the right materials to match your home's architectural style while ensuring the new installation meets modern standards for weatherproofing and insulation.

Our team, led by owner David Gohn, manages the entire process—from stripping away old, failing wood to preparing the surface and installing fresh boards with expert precision. We focus on durable, high-quality finishes that can withstand the unique coastal and city environments of Pacifica, San Francisco, and beyond. Whether you are replacing a single wall or the whole house, we deliver a seamless, polished result. Common situations: Replacing old, warped wood siding that can no longer be effectively repaired or painted.; Upgrading from damaged wood to fresh, high-quality lumber or modern alternatives.; Full siding replacement during a major home renovation or restoration project.. Related services: Wood Siding Repair, Full Siding Replacement, Siding Stripping, Siding Preparation, Exterior Siding installation, General Contractor Services. Locations served: Pacifica, Daly City, San Bruno, South San Francisco, Brisbane, Colma, Millbrae, San Mateo, Burlingame, Half Moon Bay, Montara, El Granada, Belmont, San Carlos, Redwood City, Hillsborough, Foster City, Menlo Park, Atherton, Palo Alto.
