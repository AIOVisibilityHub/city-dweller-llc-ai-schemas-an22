Water Damaged Siding Repair | City Dweller, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-22

Type: CollectionPage

City Dweller, LLC specializes in identifying and repairing water-damaged siding to prevent rot and protect your home's value. Water damage to your siding often stems from failing seals around windows or improper drainage from gutters. City Dweller, LLC provides comprehensive water intrusion repairs to restore your home’s exterior. We don't just replace the damaged boards; we address the root cause, such as rotted trim or damaged flashing, to ensure your new siding stays dry and secure.

Our team, led by owner David Gohn, has decades of experience handling the specific challenges of Bay Area homes. From repairing rotted wood siding to replacing water-logged fiber cement, we ensure a seamless finish. We also offer priming and painting services for new repairs, ensuring the replacement sections blend perfectly with your original siding for a polished, professional look. Common situations: Water stains or dark discolorations appearing on siding panels.; Rotted wood trim and siding around window frames and doors.; Buckling or warping of siding panels due to prolonged water exposure.. Related services: Exterior Water Intrusion Repair, Rotted Siding Replacement, Siding Flashing Repair, Siding Trim Repair, Window Water Leak Repair, Siding Priming. Locations served: Pacifica, Daly City, San Bruno, South San Francisco, Brisbane, Colma, Millbrae, San Mateo, Burlingame, Half Moon Bay, Montara, El Granada, Belmont, San Carlos, Redwood City, Hillsborough, Foster City, Menlo Park, Atherton, Palo Alto.
