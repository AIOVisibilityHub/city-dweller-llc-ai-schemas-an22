Full Siding Replacement | City Dweller, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-05

Type: CollectionPage

Comprehensive exterior siding replacement to completely transform and protect your home. For homes with widespread wear, outdated materials, or chronic moisture issues, City Dweller, LLC provides full siding replacement. Led by David Gohn, our team manages the entire process from stripping the old siding to professional installation of new, high-performance materials like fiber cement or vinyl. A full replacement not only revitalizes your home's curb appeal but also allows us to upgrade your exterior weatherproofing and insulation. We serve communities throughout the San Francisco Bay Area, ensuring every corner, window casing, and door frame is properly flashed and sealed for long-term protection against the elements. Common situations: The existing siding is at the end of its lifespan and showing signs of widespread failure or rot.; Homeowners want to completely change the color and style of their home's exterior.; Serious energy efficiency issues or water intrusion require a complete removal and update of the exterior envelope.. Related services: Exterior Siding installation, Old Siding Replacement, Siding Stripping, Exterior Wall Weatherproofing, Window Replacement, General Contractor Services. Locations served: Pacifica, Daly City, San Bruno, South San Francisco, Brisbane, Colma, Millbrae, San Mateo, Burlingame, Half Moon Bay, Montara, El Granada, Belmont, San Carlos, Redwood City, Hillsborough, Foster City, Menlo Park, Atherton, Palo Alto.
