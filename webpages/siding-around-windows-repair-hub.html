Siding Around Windows Repair | City Dweller, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-23

Protect your home from water intrusion with expert siding repairs around window frames by City Dweller, LLC. The area where your siding meets your window frames is one of the most critical points for preventing leaks. If you notice gaps, peeling paint, or soft spots in the siding surrounding your windows, it is time for a professional assessment. City Dweller, LLC specializes in repairing the siding and trim specifically located around window openings.

Our team, including partner Glen Hogerton with over 25 years of window and door experience, understands the complexities of proper flashing and integrated weatherproofing. We focus on ensuring that the water-shedding system around your windows is intact, preventing expensive interior damage. We work with vinyl, fiber cement, and wood siding to ensure a perfect match and a watertight seal. Common situations: Gaps forming between the window frame and siding panels.; Visible rot or soft wood on the siding trim immediately surrounding a window.; Water leaks appearing inside the home near window headers.; Loose or missing siding pieces around basement or kitchen windows.. Related services: Window Repair, Window Flashing Repair, Siding Caulking, Siding Trim Repair, Exterior Moisture Protection, Water Damaged Siding Repair. Locations served: Pacifica, Daly City, San Bruno, South San Francisco, San Mateo, Burlingame, Hillsborough, Redwood City, Palo Alto.
