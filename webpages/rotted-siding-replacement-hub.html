Rotted Siding Replacement | City Dweller, LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-22

Protect your home's structural integrity with professional rotted siding removal and replacement services from City Dweller, LLC. Wood rot in your siding is a serious threat that can spread to your home's framing if left unaddressed. City Dweller, LLC specializes in rotted siding replacement, carefully removing decayed boards and treating the area to prevent future issues. Our team identifies the source of the rot—often failed caulking or poor drainage—to ensure the new siding stays dry and durable.

We work with high-quality replacement materials, including fiber cement and treated wood, to provide long-lasting protection. Our services include not only the replacement of the damaged panels but also the repair of rotted trim, window surrounds, and door frames that are often affected simultaneously. Trust our decades of general contracting experience to restore your home's safety and value. Common situations: Soft, crumbling wood siding caused by prolonged exposure to fog and rain.; Rot developing around window sills and door frames due to failed flashing.; Fungal growth or dark staining on siding indicating advanced moisture decay.. Related services: Wood Siding Restoration, Moisture Damaged Siding Repair, Rotted Window Frame Repair, Rot Damage Around Sliding Doors, Siding Flashing Repair, Siding Sealing. Locations served: Pacifica, Daly City, San BrunoBaseboard Repair, Millbrae, Burlingame, San Mateo, Half Moon Bay, Montara, El Granada.
